Rifleman - Standard Fighter

Longshooter - range and damage, cannot use heavy weapons

Gunslinger - bonuses with pistols and scatterguns, mobility, cannot use heavy weapons

Grenadier - Armor, explosives, repeater guns, armor shredding

Logistics - healing, hacking, items in the field, cannot use heavy weapons

Guardian - armor, health, regeneration, shielding allies

Brawler - cleave, bleed, weaken, action afvantage

Jedi - dice manipulations, light sabers (pierce), healing, recovery, moving enemoes and objects, cannot use heavy weapons
